# Yellow Propaganda Style Guide  
### ljrseals-dev / Shakespeare Mystery School Edition

## Voice
- Oracular yet tabloid
- Elevated diction mixed with earthy, bodily, musical language
- Treats historical fact as the surface of deeper mythic pattern
- Music is never “entertainment”; it is intelligence, telemetry, coded survival knowledge
- The Chitlin’ Circuit is a Mystery School that operated in plain sight

## Headline Formula
ALL-CAPS or Title Case urgency.  
“THE HIDDEN SCHOOL THAT FED ON INTESTINES AND BIRTHED ROCK AND ROLL”  
“WHY THE BLUES WAS NEVER JUST MUSIC — IT WAS THE LAST FREE INTELLIGENCE NETWORK”

## Body Tactics
- Open with a sensory shock (smell of chitlins, heat of the stage, darkness of the road)
- Immediately elevate to mythic register (underworld journey, alchemical transformation, School of Night parallel)
- Insert “intelligence” framing: performers as agents, venues as nodes, the circuit as distributed mind
- Use rhetorical questions that answer themselves dramatically
- Close with a call that feels like both prophecy and sales pitch

## Forbidden
- Dry academic tone
- Neutral reportage
- Apologetic hedging
- Modern political score-settling (keep it archetypal)

## Preferred Imagery
- Intestines as labyrinth / entrails as maps of the soul
- Stage lights as stars of a forbidden constellation
- The road as pilgrimage and exile
- Harmonics as encrypted messages that white power could not decode
- Bobby Rush and the elders as living repositories of the School

## Signature Closing Cadence
Something that echoes both Shakespearean couplet and yellow-press flourish:
“The circuit never died. It only changed key. Listen closer.”

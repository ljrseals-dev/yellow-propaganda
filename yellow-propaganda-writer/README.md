# Yellow Propaganda Writer — Autonomous Narrative Engine

**ljrseals-dev style**  
Shakespearean Mystery School · Folklore · Myth · Fable · Music-as-Intelligence lens

## What This Is
An autonomous-style propaganda writer that reprocesses factual research (the Chitlin’ Circuit / chitterlings archive) through the dynamic tactics of **Yellow Propaganda**.

Yellow Propaganda draws from classical yellow journalism (scare headlines, emotional amplification, self-promotion, vivid imagery, moral drama) fused with:
- Shakespearean Mystery School (School of Night) atmospherics — hidden knowledge, coded language, intellectual cabals
- Folklore, mythology, and fable structures
- Music treated as living intelligence / signal / harmonic telemetry
- The developer’s analyzer aesthetic (president-speeches style discourse analysis refracted into mythic narrative)

## Core Tactics Employed
1. Scare / urgency headlines in oversized rhetorical print
2. Lavish sensory and musical imagery
3. Elevation of ordinary historical fact into archetypal struggle
4. Anonymous or oracular “sources”
5. Self-referential promotion of the intelligence of the circuit itself
6. Mythic reframing: the circuit as modern mystery school, the chitlin as sacrificial / alchemical food of the underworld journey
7. Harmonic intelligence: blues, R&B, and soul as encrypted knowledge transmission

## Files
- `yellow_propaganda_writer.py` — lightweight autonomous generator (template + seed story engine)
- `sample_propaganda_stories.md` — three fully written Yellow Propaganda pieces processing the Chitlin’ Circuit project
- `style_guide.md` — the living style bible
- This README

## Usage
```bash
python yellow_propaganda_writer.py
```
Or feed it new seed facts and it will spin Yellow Propaganda narratives.

## Origin Note
Built for the Chitlin’ Circuit research package. The food (chitterlings), the road (the circuit), and the music are treated as a single living intelligence system that survived segregation by encoding itself in sound and story.
